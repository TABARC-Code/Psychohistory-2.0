# Weak-Signal Propagation and Butterfly Pathways

Status: experimental architecture extension. This document does not claim empirical predictive skill.

## Purpose

Psychohistory must not discard a result merely because its subject appears trivial, culturally odd, commercially narrow, or unrelated to a currently important outcome. A weak anomaly can be either noise, a symptom of an upstream cause, or the beginning of a propagating change. The system must preserve that distinction and test it forward rather than decide importance from human intuition.

The governing question is not only "what important things are changing?" but "what apparently unimportant things are changing that could become important?"

## Weak-signal object

For anomaly a_i at time t retain the vector

W_i(t) = [A_i, R_i, B_i, C_i, S_i, P_i, Q_i]

where A is anomaly strength, R propagation/reproduction, B network breadth, C behavioural conversion, S structural connectivity, P persistence, and Q measurement quality.

Do not collapse this vector into a mandatory scalar. Any scalar summary is diagnostic only unless separately validated.

Virality != systemic importance.
Unusualness != consequence.
Correlation != propagation.

## Reservoir

All measurement-valid anomalies may enter a weak-signal reservoir even when no macro-outcome is known. Each entry carries observation time, availability time, vintage, ancestry, source, uncertainty, domains, and expiry/review rules.

Signals are not promoted because they are interesting. Most are expected to die out.

## Forward butterfly paths

For each retained anomaly construct zero or more falsifiable paths:

a_i -> X_1 -> X_2 -> ... -> X_n -> candidate outcome.

Each edge records direction, expected lag/range, mechanism class, evidence ancestry, confidence and competing common-driver explanations. The system records both observed propagation and ExpectedButAbsentPropagation.

A path loses support when expected intermediate effects fail to appear within their declared windows. It must not be repaired retrospectively by inventing new intermediate steps after the outcome.

## Inverse inference

Odd observations may be symptoms rather than causes. Run the inverse question in parallel:

observed anomaly -> candidate upstream causes -> source posterior -> forward consistency tests.

This allows a small shortage, preference shift or niche price movement to reveal a broader process without assuming that the small event itself causes the macro outcome.

## Convergence and interaction

Track independent weak signals that converge on a common node or transition:

{a_1, a_2, ..., a_k} -> X.

Convergence gains evidential value only after Evidence Ancestry checks. Multiple measurements derived from one underlying event remain one evidence family.

Also retain interactions in which individually weak signals amplify one another. These are candidate nonlinear pathways, not automatically admitted causal effects.

## Model disagreement

Every applicable registered algorithm and sidecar may evaluate a weak signal. Preserve the vector of outputs and pairwise disagreement rather than averaging it away.

A model may be useful because it predicts an outcome, because it identifies a source, because it detects propagation, or because its disagreement with another model predicts regime change. These roles must be scored separately.

## Prediction versus validation

The prediction engine is allowed to issue speculative probabilities and scenario branches before aggregate Psychohistory skill is established. Scientific validation remains conservative.

Prediction:
P(X_{t+h} | F_t)

Evaluation:
score only forecasts frozen before the outcome, against their declared Outcome Contract and baselines.

Experimental predictions must therefore be timestamped and immutable, but lack of prior calibration is not by itself grounds to suppress a forecast. It must instead be labelled uncalibrated/developmental.

## Promotion logic

A weak signal may rise in attention when independent evidence shows propagation, persistence, behavioural conversion, structural reach, convergent signals, or successful expected intermediate effects. It falls when propagation fails, ancestry collapses apparent breadth, the anomaly revises away, a common driver explains it better, or prospective tests fail.

No fixed universal threshold is asserted here. Thresholds must be learned or prospectively evaluated rather than invented.

## Anti-storytelling constraints

Human plausibility cannot delete an odd result. Human novelty cannot promote one.

News prose cannot create a causal edge.
Retrospective narrative cannot repair a failed path.
A bizarre prediction remains in the ledger if it was validly generated.
A compelling explanation remains exploratory until tested.
Missing expected propagation is negative evidence.

The desired behaviour is therefore: retain -> connect -> predict -> watch propagation -> freeze -> resolve -> score -> update future model status.
