# Abstract

We present Psychohistory 2.1, a probabilistic science of civilisational dynamics built from two complementary formalisms. R3 models population, resources, institutions, and competing narratives on a manifold or graph with adaptive resonance and path-dependent Crossroads criteria. VHP introduces a social free-energy functional whose gradient-flow dynamics yield a regime-transition hazard through loss of convexity. A generational sidecar treats Strauss–Howe-style cycles as weak, noisy confirmatory evidence rather than prophecy. The framework supplies observables, estimation, validation, governance, and implementation guidance for early warning, scenario generation, and low-regret intervention design.

# 1. Introduction

Psychohistory 2.1 aims to measure when systems are primed to jump rather than predict destiny. Narratives, resources, institutions, and topology co-evolve; the result is a warning system, not a command system.

# 2. Core Architecture

R3 explains propagation through coupled fields. VHP explains event geometry through a learned free-energy landscape. GCCM provides a sidecar confirmation layer based on cohort composition and a latent slow phase.

# 4. Measurement and Validation

Narratives from embeddings and sentiment, population from mobility and participation, resources from energy and affordability indices, institutions from throughput and trust, topology from privacy-preserving multilayer graphs. Score with Brier/CRPS, reliability diagrams, lead-time distributions, and ablations against momentum/VAR/Hawkes baselines.

# 5. Ethics and Governance

No individual targeting. Publish model cards, data sheets, risk logs, and audit trails. Use an ethics veto when an apparently effective intervention would undermine autonomy or fairness.

# 6. Conclusion

The framework is designed to reduce avoidable collapse, improve graceful pivots, and make uncertainty legible. It offers warning rather than prophecy, and disciplined scenario analysis rather than mysticism.
