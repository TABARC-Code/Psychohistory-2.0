# B.1 Purpose

This appendix specifies the fields, tables, transforms, governance artefacts, and QA gates needed to run Psychohistory 2.1 reproducibly and defensibly.

# B.2 Minimal schema

Narratives: embeddings, topics, sentiment, reliability, region, time. Population: mobility, participation, turnout. Resources: energy, staples, affordability. Institutions: throughput, trust, backlog, fiscal capacity. Topology: multilayer graph snapshots with privacy budgets. Instability: curvature, gradients, entropy, slack, sigma. Sidecar: cohort shares and phase theta.

# B.3 Measurement pipeline

Clean and deduplicate text, embed, weight by source credibility, and aggregate by region and time. Harmonise mobility and participation, build affordability-adjusted resource baskets, estimate institutional throughput and trust, and construct privacy-preserving graphs for communications, logistics, and finance layers.

# B.4 Privacy and QA

Apply differential privacy where required, maintain lineage and snapshot hashes, monitor drift with PSI/KL divergence, and publish model cards plus data sheets. No individual targeting is permitted.
