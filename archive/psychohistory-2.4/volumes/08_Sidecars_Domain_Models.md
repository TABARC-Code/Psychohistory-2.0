*Markets, geopolitics, climate, technology, corporate, supply chain and candidate domains*

Psychohistory 2.4 | Canonical storage revision | 6 October 2026

# 1. Sidecar interface
Sidecar_d(O_t,T_E) -> {Signal_d, Vector_d, Hazard_d?}.
Hazard is absent unless tied to a calibrated Outcome Contract. Sidecars may disagree; disagreement is retained.

# 2. BADL/BADL-M
O=(S^0.30 C^0.20 N^0.15 T^0.15 A^0.20)/(R^0.60 F^0.40).
Legacy handcrafted Cobb-Douglas-style outlier score. Exponents are modelling choices and it must compete against robust covariance, Mahalanobis, z-score and supervised baselines.

# 3. GISC
Geopolitical instability uses observable events, official actions, mobilisation, sanctions, trade disruption and exposure. Media volume is not incidence; syndicated reporting is ancestry-linked.

# 4. CTSC
Separates physical hazard, policy transition, energy-system change, financial exposure and behavioural response. Short and long horizons are not merged.

# 5. TPSS
Separates capability, investment, adoption, substitution and institutional response. Patent counts, funding and benchmark capability are different observation systems.

# 6. CCSC
Separates accounting deterioration, market-implied distress, liquidity/refinancing exposure, supplier/customer propagation and formal insolvency.

# 7. SCFS
PaymentValue_ij,t approx Price_ij,t * Quantity_ij,t * TimingFactor_ij,t * ContractFactor_ij,t.
Payment values cannot be relabelled physical flow. Network features must beat prices/freight/sector baselines.

# 8. Infectious disease candidate
ObservedCases_t approx Incidence_t * DetectionProbability_t + reporting_error_t.
Testing policy and detection probability vary. Epidemiological renewal/SEIR-type models are mandatory baselines for outbreak tasks.

# 9. Banking/lending/property
B0: price persistence -> B1: approvals -> B2: approvals+rates -> PH additions.
Recognised leading indicators consumed by PH must also appear in the baseline.

# 10. Gold
Central-bank purchases, ETF/fund flows, futures positioning and retail demand are separate mechanisms. A purchase rise is not assigned a single motive.

# 11. Pensions
Contributions, withdrawals, transfers and allocation changes are distinct flows/stocks with demographic, tax, income and regulatory drivers.

# 12. Arts/cultural activity
Operationalise attendance, revenue, production, employment, venue openings/closures, publication, streaming or funding. Archival survival bias is explicit.

# 13. Disability hostility/violence candidate
Recorded_t=f(Incidence_t,Reporting_t,Recording_t,LegalDefinition_t,Enforcement_t,Classification_t).
No scalar hostility index is admitted. Police, prosecution, victimisation, service reports, online abuse and news are separate observation processes.

# 14. Shock geometry
SG_t=[Delta x/sigma_Delta x, speed, persistence, breadth, response_vector].
Shock -> Exposure -> Response.
Buffers can delay/suppress/redirect transmission. Stress Absorption remains conceptual until scales are identified.
