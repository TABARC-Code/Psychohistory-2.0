# First frozen real forecast

Forecast **PH24-UK-HOUSING-0001** was frozen on 7 October 2026 before the Bank of England's scheduled 29 October 2026 Money and Credit release.

The event is whether seasonally adjusted net mortgage approvals for house purchase in September 2026 are below the August 2026 first-release value of 54,900.

This forecast is deliberately classified as a **development candidate**, not an admitted Psychohistory production forecast. Its probability (0.5239487262) is generated from a simple OLS time trend fitted to the January-August 2026 first-release approval sequence, with a Student-t one-step predictive distribution. The baseline probability is 0.50, the observed fraction of month-to-month declines across the available January-August 2026 sequence.

The purpose of the forecast is not to claim predictive success. It establishes the first immutable prospective record against which the framework can be scored. The first-release September value is the scoring target. Later revisions must be retained separately and cannot replace the scoring vintage.

The transaction stage is explicitly **approval**. Drawdown, gross lending, repayment and mortgage-stock observations are related pipeline observations, not duplicate confirmations of the same forecast.
